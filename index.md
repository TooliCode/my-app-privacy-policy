# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 2026

Thank you for using our Windows application. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information.

## 1. Data Collection and Storage
* **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) on external servers or to the developer.
* **Local Processing:** All configurations, settings, and files created or modified within the app are stored exclusively on your local device.

## 2. External Data Transfer & Third-Party Services
* **Export via Third-Party Services:** The application allows you to export or share encoded files/data directly with third-party messaging services (such as WhatsApp). 
* **User Initiation:** Any data transfer to third-party platforms is initiated solely by you (the user).
* **Third-Party Policies:** Once data is transferred to a third-party service like WhatsApp, the privacy policy and terms of service of that specific provider apply. We have no access to or control over the data processed by these external platforms.

## 3. Analytics and Tracking
* Our Windows application does not use any third-party analytics tools, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.

## 4. Permissions
* The application requests only the minimum system permissions necessary to function properly on your device (e.g., local file system access for saving configurations).
